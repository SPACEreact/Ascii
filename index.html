DEPTH & CAMERA

Flat plane

Use Drag camera, Raise depth or Lower depth on the canvas. Small camera moves work best: hidden surfaces are not reconstructed.

AI depth runs locally using Depth Anything V2 Small. First use downloads a model and runtime from Hugging Face / jsDelivr. Photos stay on your device. Video depth is a frozen frame, not tracked geometry.

LIGHT LAB

Paint light to create up to eight local emitters. Depth normals approximate lighting; shadows and light already baked into your photo remain. Transparent photo mode preserves the source alpha and skips lighting overlays.

MOTION DESIGN

Pick a recipe. Paint motion limits it to a region; Pin keeps an area still.

Suggestions use colour and foreground contrast, not AI object recognition. Paint the exact area when foreground detection needs help.
